# Example videos: attribution

- `train-viaduct_teacher_vs_student.mp4`: derived from `train-viaduct.mp4` (same author and licence): two copies side by side with the masks of SAM 2.1 and sam2-lite.
- `swans.mp4`: "Trumpeter swan family.webm" by Lorie Shaull, [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0), from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trumpeter_swan_family.webm (seconds 4-9).
